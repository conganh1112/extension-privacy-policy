Privacy Policy - 1688 Order Assistant

Last Updated: December 2024
Extension Name: 1688 Order Assistant
Version: 1.0.0

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the 1688 Order Assistant Chrome extension ("we", "our", or "the Extension") handles information when you use our service. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we process your data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Order Data

The Extension collects and processes the following information from 1688.com:

  • Product information (SKU, name, description)
  • Order quantities and specifications
  • Pricing information
  • Order form data

2.2 What We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or process:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone)
  • Payment information or credit card details
  • Account credentials or passwords
  • Browsing history outside of 1688.com
  • Personal communications or messages

3. How We Use Your Information

The Extension uses collected data exclusively for:

  • Automating order processing on 1688.com
  • Extracting product information for order management
  • Communicating with your order management system
  • Streamlining your shopping workflow

4. Data Storage and Processing

4.1 Local Processing

All data processing occurs locally within your browser. Order information is temporarily stored in browser memory only during active use of the Extension.

4.2 Server Communication

The Extension communicates with your specified servers only:

  • Your local development server (localhost:3000)
  • Your production order management system

No data is transmitted to our servers or any third-party services.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, or disclose your information to any third parties. All data remains within your control and is only shared with your specified order management system.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • All data processing occurs locally in your browser
  • No data is stored on external servers
  • Communication is limited to your specified systems only
  • No persistent storage of sensitive information

7. Your Rights and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • You can disable the Extension at any time
  • You control which servers the Extension communicates with
  • No data is stored permanently
  • You can revoke permissions at any time

8. Extension Permissions

The Extension requires the following permissions:

  • activeTab: To access 1688.com pages for order processing
  • scripting: To inject helper scripts for data communication
  • tabs: To coordinate between 1688.com and your management system

9. Third-Party Services

The Extension does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools. All functionality is self-contained and operates only with your specified systems.

10. Children's Privacy

This Extension is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the Extension's data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: anhpc1112@gmail.com
  • Extension Support: Through Chrome Web Store
This Privacy Policy is effective as of the date listed above and applies to all users of the 1688 Order Assistant Chrome extension.
